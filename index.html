Lesson Schedule – Privacy Policy

App: Lesson Schedule / გაკვეთილების ცხრილი (Android package and iOS bundle identifier com.ggvg.schoollessons)
Developer: GGVG · Contact: v.gigilashvili@gmail.com
Last updated: 12 November 2026

In short

Lesson Schedule is a school timetable and homework app. It works fully without an account, and in that case everything you enter stays on your device. The app has no advertising and no analytics or tracking. If you choose to sign in with Google in the Android app, a copy of your data is saved to a cloud database so you can use it on another device.

iPhone and iPad

The iPhone and iPad app has no sign-in and no cloud copy. It does not collect any data: everything you enter (lessons, timetable, homework notes, profile, settings and an optional profile photo) is stored only on your device and is never sent to us or to anyone else. The app contains no advertising, no analytics, no tracking and no third-party SDKs, and it does not use the advertising identifier (IDFA). The sections below that describe signing in, the cloud copy and the QR scanner apply to the Android app only.

What the app stores

DataWhere it is kept
Lessons, timetable, bell times, holidays, sick days, homework ticks and notesOn your device. Also in the cloud copy if you sign in.
Profile: name, school name, grade, optional school links, chosen avatar or profile photoOn your device. Also in the cloud copy if you sign in.
App settings (language, theme, reminder, sound)On your device. Also in the cloud copy if you sign in.
Google account identifier, name and email addressAndroid app only, and only if you sign in. Held by Firebase Authentication to recognise your account.
Firebase installation identifier (a random ID created by the Firebase libraries in the app)Android app only. Used only so that sign-in and the cloud copy can work. It is not used for advertising or tracking. The app does not use the Android advertising ID.

All profile fields are optional. A nickname works just as well as a real name.

Sign-in and cloud copy (optional, Android only)

If you tap “Sign in with Google”, the app uses Google's Firebase Authentication to sign you in and Cloud Firestore to store one document containing the data listed above. This document is stored in Google data centres in the European Union and can be read and changed only by the signed-in account it belongs to. It is used for one purpose: restoring and synchronising your own data between your devices. We do not use it for anything else and do not share or sell it.

Google acts as our service provider for these features. See Privacy and Security in Firebase.

Photos, camera and notifications

  • Photo picker is used only when you choose to set a profile photo. The photo is shrunk to a small square and stored as part of your profile on your device (and in the cloud copy on Android if you sign in). The app does not read your photo library; on iPhone and iPad you pick a single photo with the system's own picker.
  • QR scanner (Android app only) is used only when you choose to copy a classmate's timetable. Nothing from the camera is stored.
  • Notifications are created on your device to remind you about the next day's lessons. They are not sent from a server. On iPhone and iPad, iOS asks for your permission first; you can change it in Settings at any time.

Sharing a timetable

The “Share timetable” screen shows a QR code containing only lesson names, lesson icons, teacher names, bell times, school days and the weekly schedule. It never contains homework, notes, your profile or your account. It leaves your device only when someone scans it from your screen. The same information can also be sent as a link: when you tap “Send as a link”, your phone's share menu opens and the link travels only through the messaging app you choose. It is not sent to us or to any server of ours.

Children

The app is designed for pupils aged 9 and older. It does not require an account, shows no advertising, uses no analytics and does not use the advertising ID. Signing in with Google is optional, and without it no personal information leaves the device. We recommend that a parent or guardian decides whether a child signs in, and that the sign-in uses the parent's account or a supervised child account. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 9. The iPhone and iPad app collects no personal information from anyone. A parent or guardian can ask us to review or delete a child's cloud data at any time using the contact address above.

Deleting your data

  • Data on the device: uninstall the app (on Android you can also clear its data in settings). On iPhone and iPad, deleting the app removes everything it stored, and there is nothing else to delete because no account or cloud copy exists.
  • Account and cloud copy, in the Android app: open More → your profile → Cloud backup → “Delete account”. This removes the cloud document and the account. Data on the device is kept until you remove it.
  • Account and cloud copy, without the app: email v.gigilashvili@gmail.com from the address you signed in with, and we will delete the account and its cloud copy within 30 days.

Signing out stops synchronisation but does not delete the cloud copy.

Security

Data travels between the app and the cloud over encrypted connections, and access rules allow each signed-in user to reach only their own document.

Links you open

If you save a school link in your profile and tap it, it opens in your web browser. That website is run by someone else and has its own privacy policy.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date.
