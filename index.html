Lesson Schedule – Privacy Policy

App: Lesson Schedule / გაკვეთილების ცხრილი (package com.ggvg.schoollessons)
Developer: GGVG · Contact: v.gigilashvili@gmail.com
Last updated: 11 November 2026

In short

Lesson Schedule is a school timetable and homework app. It works fully without an account, and in that case everything you enter stays on your device. The app has no advertising and no analytics or tracking. If you choose to sign in with Google, a copy of your data is saved to a cloud database so you can use it on another device.

What the app stores

DataWhere it is kept
Lessons, timetable, bell times, holidays, sick days, homework ticks and notesOn your device. Also in the cloud copy if you sign in.
Profile: name, school name, grade, optional school links, chosen avatar or profile photoOn your device. Also in the cloud copy if you sign in.
App settings (language, theme, reminder, sound)On your device. Also in the cloud copy if you sign in.
Google account identifier, name and email addressOnly if you sign in. Held by Firebase Authentication to recognise your account.

All profile fields are optional. A nickname works just as well as a real name.

Sign-in and cloud copy (optional)

If you tap “Sign in with Google”, the app uses Google's Firebase Authentication to sign you in and Cloud Firestore to store one document containing the data listed above. This document is stored in Google data centres in the European Union and can be read and changed only by the signed-in account it belongs to. It is used for one purpose: restoring and synchronising your own data between your devices. We do not use it for anything else and do not share or sell it.

Google acts as our service provider for these features. See Privacy and Security in Firebase.

Camera, photos and notifications

  • Camera and photo picker are used only when you choose to set a profile photo. The photo is shrunk to a small square and stored as part of your profile. The app does not read your photo library.
  • QR scanner is used only when you choose to copy a classmate's timetable. Nothing from the camera is stored.
  • Notifications are created on your device to remind you about the next day's lessons. They are not sent from a server.

Sharing a timetable

The “Share timetable” screen shows a QR code containing only lesson names, lesson icons, teacher names, bell times, school days and the weekly schedule. It never contains homework, notes, your profile or your account. It leaves your device only when someone scans it from your screen.

Children

The app is suitable for school pupils of all ages. It does not require an account, shows no advertising and does not track users. We recommend that a parent or guardian decides whether a child signs in, and that the sign-in uses the parent's account or a supervised child account. A parent or guardian can ask us to delete a child's cloud data at any time using the contact address above.

Deleting your data

  • Data on the device: uninstall the app, or clear its data in Android settings.
  • Account and cloud copy, in the app: open More → your profile → Cloud backup → “Delete account”. This removes the cloud document and the account. Data on the device is kept until you remove it.
  • Account and cloud copy, without the app: email v.gigilashvili@gmail.com from the address you signed in with, and we will delete the account and its cloud copy within 30 days.

Signing out stops synchronisation but does not delete the cloud copy.

Security

Data travels between the app and the cloud over encrypted connections, and access rules allow each signed-in user to reach only their own document.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date.
